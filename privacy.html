EvenPair Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026. Publisher: Osama Alfityani.

EvenPair is an offline puzzle game. Version 1.0.0 has no player accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, gameplay analytics or developer-operated crash reporting. Advertising, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled in this release.

Information on your device

Puzzle progress, results, times, play dates, statistics and preferences are stored locally. They are not automatically sent to the publisher. Device backups may include this information according to your Apple or Google account settings. EvenPair does not operate a cloud-sync service.

Reset local progress clears gameplay progress, results, statistics and gameplay settings. Uninstalling removes the local application database, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behaviour. Manage backups separately through your device settings.

Optional device features

Local reminders are scheduled only after you enable them and grant notification permission. The app uses your device timezone to schedule reminders; it does not request your location. Reminders and haptic feedback can be disabled in Settings.

Copy result places the selected result on your system clipboard. Your device settings may allow clipboard contents to sync to your other devices.

Privacy and support websites

These pages open in your browser and are hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and browser/request information under its own privacy policy. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or website cookies.

See GitHub’s privacy statement for its hosting practices.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, the publisher receives the information you send and uses it to investigate and respond to your request. Gmail and your chosen email provider process correspondence under their own terms and privacy policies. Saved puzzles and device files are not automatically attached or sent.

Resolved support correspondence is retained for 90 days after resolution, unless a longer period is legally required. Contact osamafitiani2001@gmail.com to request help or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Changes

Any future change to advertising or information collection will require an updated application and privacy disclosure. This policy will show its updated effective date.
