EvenPair Support

Two shapes. One balanced grid.

How to play

Fill the 6×6 board with circles and diamonds. Every row and column needs three of each. Avoid three matching shapes together in a line. An = link means its cells match; a × link means they differ.

Tap an editable cell to cycle through circle, diamond and empty. Given cells stay fixed. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. Undo returns a move; Clear removes your entries and can be undone. Hints remain unlimited and free. A hint counts when requested, including one you dismiss; solution reveals are clearly labelled.

Saved progress and timing

Continue your unfinished puzzle from Home. Active timing pauses away from the board, during blocking dialogs and while the app is in the background. If a save warning appears, keep the app open and choose Retry. Avoid deleting local data to resolve a temporary save warning.

Progress stays on the device. There is no publisher-operated account or cloud sync. Reset local progress removes gameplay records and settings. Operating-system backups may restore local data.

Reminders and feedback

Reminders are optional local notifications. Enable them in Settings and grant permission if you want them. Delivery times can be affected by Focus, battery and notification settings. You can keep reminders off or turn off haptic feedback.

Advertising and privacy

Version 1.0.0 has no advertisements, purchases, gameplay analytics or developer-operated crash reporting. Read the privacy policy for local storage, backups, website hosting and support correspondence.
